SeaVolt rig - printed-only construction kit
===========================================
NOTE: The parametric generator scripts referenced below were recovered/rebuilt
on 2026-08-25:
  * seavolt_rig.py      -> regenerates the 6 frame parts (hub, strut, leg, foot,
                          pump_clamp, shelf). Tune LIP / SHELF_LIP / GRIP, then
                          re-run to re-export the STLs.
  * reactor_chamber.py  -> regenerates the 5 reaction-core parts (chamber_stand,
                          chamber_body, chamber_lid, al_cartridge_basket,
                          al_cartridge_lid) with functional seawater/H2/drain
                          ports, perforated basket, heat fins, and an O-ring lid.
Run:  python seavolt_rig.py   /   python reactor_chamber.py
Both scripts self-verify (bounding box + single-solid check) on each run.

All joints are radial press-fits, no glue, no bolts, no inserts:
  plug 6.00 mm  ->  snaps past a 5.85 mm lip  ->  seats in a 6.15 mm slot.

FILAMENT
  PETG strongly recommended (press-fits; PLA is brittle and can crack on
  insertion). If you must use PLA, loosen the fit first (see TUNING).

PRINT SETTINGS
  0.2 mm layers, 3 perimeters, 15-20% infill is plenty for the frame.
  Use an organic/gyroid infill in the slicer - it saves material over grid
  while staying strong. The legs (tapered octagonal "bone") and the shelf
  (spoked deck) are already lightened; keep the perimeters around the snap
  lips and plug sockets solid (3 perimeters or 100% for the first 2 mm of
  every joint region). One part per plate. All parts print flat, no supports.

PART LIST (print once per listed quantity; ~340 g PETG total)
  kit_hub         x3   disc flat, slots facing up
  kit_strut_128   x3   triangle edges, laid flat on a side
  kit_leg         x3   X-pattern legs, laid flat on a side (bone profile)
  kit_foot        x3   flat
  kit_pump_clamp  x1   tube upright (split + stub clear of the plate)
  kit_shelf       x1   flat, snap keyways on the underside + raised snap
                       collar on top (spoked deck)

  Optional containers (print as-is, watertight):
  chamber_stand   x1   flat, no supports
  chamber_body    x1   (needs supports or a small raft under the side inlet
                       boss and drain boss if printing in PLA)
  chamber_lid     x1   O-ring groove on the underside; seals the chamber for
                      pressure-rated H2 collection
  al_cartridge_basket   x1   perforated walls + base; central vent tube
  al_cartridge_lid      x1

TUNING (if the snap is wrong, adjust BEFORE printing a full set)
  Open seavolt_prototype\seavolt_rig.py and change LIP near the top.
    * joints too loose / fall apart:  lower LIP by 0.05 (tighter snap)
    * joints won't click together:    raise LIP by 0.05
  Shelf-to-bar snap is tuned separately with SHELF_LIP (default 9.90 mm,
  i.e. 0.10 mm interference with the 10 mm bar - gentler than the plugs
  because three mouths have to seat at once).
    * shelf pops off / too easy:   lower SHELF_LIP by 0.05
    * shelf won't seat over bars:  raise SHELF_LIP by 0.05
  The stand-to-shelf collar snap (0.2 mm interference, skirt pockets) and the
  chamber-to-stand ring snap (0.15 mm interference, waist groove) are fixed
  geometry - if they fight, tune them in the code: the collar bumps in
  seavolt_rig.py (shelf(), "Raised snap collar") and the ring bumps + groove
  in reactor_chamber.py (build_stand / build_chamber "Snap waist").
  Re-run the module(s) to re-export the STLs.

ASSEMBLY (3-legged triangular frame, bench-top)
  1. Take one hub. Press a strut plug into a slot until it clicks. The plug
     goes in straight (radially) - push the BAR, not the plug tip.
  2. Add the next hub onto the strut's free end, then complete the triangle
     with the remaining struts. You now have a rigid triangle of 3 hubs +
     3 bars.
  3. Push an X leg up through each hub's centre socket from underneath until
     it clicks. Each leg leans outward past its neighbour, so the feet land
     on the 60-deg-rotated foot triangle (1.2x the hub radius) and the legs
     brace one another. Press a foot onto the bottom of each leg.
  4. Plug the pump clamp's stub into one hub's outward slot (the third slot,
     the one that points away from the triangle). Open the split collar and
     slide the pump in; the collar grips around the pump body.
  5. Press the shelf onto the three bars so its snap keyways click past the
     bar tops (one click per bar). To take it back off, push up on two bars
     at once - the mouth releases with a click.
  6. Press the chamber stand (chamber_stand.stl) down onto the shelf until
     its skirt clicks over the collar (four bumps, one firm push - the skirt
     flexes out 0.2 mm and the bumps seat in the skirt pockets). Lift by
     pulling straight up on the rim.
  7. Seat the chamber body in the stand: drop it in and press down until the
     ring's three bumps click into the waist groove (the body cams past them
     at 0.15 mm). It pulls straight back out. Fill the cartridge with the
     aluminum charge, drop it in the chamber, snap the cartridge lid on.

REAL-WORLD STRUCTURAL TEST
  * Joint wiggle: with the rig loaded (chamber + water, ~2 kg), grab a bar
    and twist. Expect no rotation in the plane of the triangle; some flex
    out of plane is normal.
  * Leg splay: push the rig sideways at the top. The X-pattern legs (each
    leaning outward to its own foot) should hold the frame square.
  * Pull-out: lift the loaded shelf by one hub. The snap lips should hold
    every joint.
  * Pump grip: the collar should keep the pump from sliding when the rig is
    nudged. If it spins, shim with tape or tighten the grip in the code
    (grip=0.2 default in pump_clamp()).

FAULTS AND FIXES
  - A joint clicks but slides out easily -> tighten LIP by 0.05.
  - Struts won't seat fully -> clean flash off the slot, or loosen LIP.
  - Hub won't take a leg -> leg plug is fine; press harder past the lip, it
    seats with the bar face flush against the hub underside.
  - Shelf snaps on one bar but not the other two -> seat one bar at a time,
    rocking the shelf; if it still fights, loosen SHELF_LIP by 0.05.
  - Stand won't click onto the shelf -> align it so the bumps line up with
    the skirt pockets (any 90 deg rotation works); a firm one-handed push
    seats it. If it still binds, shave 0.05 mm off the collar bump outer
    radius in seavolt_rig.py.
  - Chamber won't click into the stand -> it is a straight push; if the ring
    binds, reduce the ring bump interference (0.15 -> 0.10) in
    reactor_chamber.py build_stand.
  - Pump collar cracked on insertion -> print in PETG, and open the split
    gap by hand a little before sliding the pump in.
